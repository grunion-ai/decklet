# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the deck, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and take criticism about **the work**: the model, the layout, the test, the trade-off.
- Assume the other person has a reason you have not heard yet, and ask.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer to a proposal. decklet is deliberately small, and most good ideas belong in a fork or a separate tool.
- Keep discussion in the issue or pull request where the work lives, so the next reader can follow it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind, in any channel.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a thread after a maintainer has asked to move on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders from the repo. Report a problem to the maintainers through GitHub: open an issue, or use the repo's private vulnerability report form when it should stay confidential. Reports are read by a person.

## Scope

This applies to the repo, its issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
